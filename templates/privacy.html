Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect basic information such as your name and email address. As you use the app, we also store the expense records you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to provide and improve the Spendly service — for example, to display your spending history, generate category breakdowns, and keep your account secure. We do not use your financial data for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely and is only accessible to you through your authenticated account. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. We may use trusted third-party services (such as hosting or infrastructure providers) solely to operate the app, and only to the extent necessary to provide the service.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on the app.
